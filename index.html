FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services does Northlight Studio offer?

Northlight Studio offers three services: brand identity and strategy, digital design and websites, and creative campaigns.

How do I start a project?

Send an enquiry using the form on this page. Your request becomes a lead for our sales team and a ticket for our IT help desk, and we reply to normal enquiries within three working days.

What happens to an urgent enquiry?

Enquiries marked urgent are given the highest priority and flagged to our on-call team with a one-hour response target.

Is Northlight Studio a real company?

No. Northlight Studio is a fictional company created for an IT learning project. All people, clients and projects are invented, and no email is actually sent from this site.
